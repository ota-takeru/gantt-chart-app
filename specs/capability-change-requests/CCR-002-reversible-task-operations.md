# Capability Change Request: CCR-002-reversible-task-operations

- Affected capabilities/versions: `focus-work-lifecycle` 1.1, `work-session-history` 1.0, `history-projection` 1.0, and `hierarchical-task-management` 1.0
- Why the locked contracts are insufficient: They deliberately exclude task deletion and post-commit undo, require completed work and prior sessions/events to remain retained, and therefore cannot satisfy the explicit request to remove mistaken tasks from history, restore a deletion, and undo committed task operations.
- Proposed semantic change: Add the `reversible-task-operations` 1.0 capability. Explicit subtree deletion removes its task/session/event records from ordinary retained history while a bounded internal undo snapshot remains. Undo may restore or remove the row images produced by the latest reversible operation. Outside explicit delete/undo transactions, all existing append-only and projection rules remain unchanged.
- Affected scenarios/states/errors/invariants: Task creation, rename, hierarchy move, completion, reopen, subtree deletion, lifecycle/session/event retention, source projections, optimistic concurrency, and revision monotonicity. Adds LIFO undo states and `undo-not-available`, `stale-undo`, and `undo-conflict` errors.
- Existing and new tests: All existing locked tests remain unchanged. New tests cover leaf/subtree deletion, absence from every ordinary projection, full restoration, create/rename/move/complete/reopen undo, mixed LIFO undo, restart persistence, bounded retention, stale tokens, conflicts, and atomic rollback.
- Compatibility or migration impact: Additive SQLite journal tables with no historical backfill. Only operations committed after migration are undoable. Ordinary APIs stay source-compatible; delete, undo-status, and undo commands are additive. Internal undo snapshots are not ordinary task history and are pruned to 50 retained entries.
- Requested authorization: Permit the new capability to make explicit deletion and undo the sole exceptions to prior no-deletion/append-only retention rules while preserving every unaffected invariant and test.
- Authorization: approved by the user's explicit 2026-08-23 request for deletion without history, deletion restoration, and undo of each task operation.
